How it works

A queue, a send, a promise.

Three things happen every morning without you. Averin decides the order, writes the words and records what came back.

Priority queue

Who gets chased first.

Every account is scored on the amount owed and how long it has been outstanding. The large invoice that is sixty days late sits above the small one that is two weeks late, every time.

  • Score = amount × log of days overdue
  • Re-ranked after every reply and payment
  • Accounts under R500 stay out of the queue
See what one run produces

Written and sent

The email you never had to write.

Each follow-up goes out under your name and your address, with the reference, the amount and the days overdue filled in. Polite on the first pass, firmer on the second. Replies are read and the sentiment surfaced to you.

  • Courtesy notice, then a formal demand, then a call
  • English, Zulu, Afrikaans, Xhosa, Sotho and Tswana
  • Stops the moment an account is paid
What you still control

Promises tracked

Every promise, on record.

When a debtor commits to a date, Averin writes it down, reminds you the day before it lapses and flags the ones that break. The live stream shows each send and each outcome as it happens.

  • Promise to pay, arrangement, dispute, no answer
  • Disputes are routed to you, never argued by software
  • A report lands in your inbox when the run finishes

Control

Automatic is not unaccountable.

It sends without you. It does not act outside the rules you set, and it never hides what it did.

Your rules
Tone, cadence, the gap between escalations and the ceiling on how far an account goes before it reaches a human.
Stop anything
Pause an account, a client or the whole run. A paid invoice drops out of the queue on its own.
Lawful contact
Follow-ups go out in your name, state the purpose up front, offer the right to dispute and keep to lawful contact hours. Calls say they are recorded. No legal threats unless you authorise them.
Audit log
Every send, call and outcome is timestamped and append-only. Export it whenever a client or a partner asks.
Where your data sits
Your book is held with a cloud host outside South Africa under a POPIA section 72 transfer clause. An operator agreement drafted for section 21 is ready for your attorney.

Wholesale distributor · 12 accounts

Live run

R469 900 committed before lunch.

A R1.25 million book, twelve accounts, fourteen to seventy-one days overdue. Averin scored the book, sent twelve emails in the client's name, followed each with WhatsApp and called every account that stayed quiet.

Eleven were reached. Seven committed to a date, four in full and three on instalments. One number failed to connect and was flagged for a human. The largest account, seventy-one days out at R134 000, agreed to a three-month arrangement on the call.
